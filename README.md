# Distribution System

**English summary.** A go-to-market plan (v0, 20 Nov 2026, discussion draft)
for Quantpet, an AI trading-bot product. It sets out an AI-assisted
distribution system: find leads, score them, reach them with the right hook,
give each one an owner, follow up, and automate only the steps that repeat.
The doc is in Vietnamese; here is what it covers.

- **Principle:** run one small loop by hand plus AI with 30 people first; automate what repeats; do not build a platform up front.
- **Goal and milestones:** Milestone 1 = 100 power users who match the ICP (100 in the CRM with an owner, 30 tried the product, 10 with steady weekly volume). End state = a system that runs weekly without depending on one person.
- **ICP:** 4 segments: bot point-farmers, perp whales, trading/farming KOLs, small funds and market makers; exchanges as a separate B2B track.
- **6 workstreams with dependencies:** framework, real performance numbers (cost per $1M volume), leads, hooks and KOLs, exchange deals (B2B), automation.
- **One loop in 8 steps:** each step produces the input of the next. Real numbers → ICP and lead sheet → social circle map → hook kit → be seen before outreach → DM and funnel tracking → automate the repeats → repeat with 100 people.
- **Open decisions:** KOL and power-user offer, CRM platform (sheet + scripts, Clay, Attio), first venue.
- **Appendices:** detail per workstream and a quest tree of what to do first.
